==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Head Injuries

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Head injuries range from minor bumps to severe traumatic brain injuries. They are dangerous because internal bleeding or swelling within the skull can compress brain tissue, leading to permanent neurological damage or death. Immediate evaluation is critical if red flag symptoms appear.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Loss of consciousness for any duration
Severe headache or vomiting
Confusion, dizziness, or slurred speech
Blood or clear fluid draining from the nose or ears
Pupils of unequal size

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Keep the victim completely still with the head and spine aligned.
2. Apply direct pressure to any bleeding scalp wound with a clean cloth.
3. Monitor the victim's level of consciousness and breathing.
4. Keep the victim warm using a clean blanket.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not wash a deep head wound as it may introduce infection or dislodge clots.
Do not remove any helmet if a neck or spinal injury is suspected.
Do not let the victim sleep if they show signs of confusion.
Do not shake the victim to wake them up.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if the victim loses consciousness.
Call if the victim experience repeated vomiting or seizures.
Call if there is clear fluid draining from the nose or ears.
Call if the injury resulted from a high-speed accident or fall.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the victim calm and lying down.
Observe the victim closely for changes in alertness or speech.
Control minor bleeding with light pressure.
Be prepared to perform CPR if the victim stops breathing.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Wear helmets when riding bicycles, motorcycles, or skateboards.
Use seatbelts in motor vehicles at all times.
Secure rugs and install safety gates on stairs.
Use protective headgear in contact sports.
