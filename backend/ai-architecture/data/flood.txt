==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Action for Floods

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Floods are overflows of water that submerge land that is usually dry. They are highly dangerous because fast-moving water can sweep away people and vehicles, trigger landslides, and contaminate drinking water. Immediate movement to high ground is critical.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Rapidly rising water levels in streets or buildings
Rushing water with debris flowing down roads
Warnings issued by local weather and disaster authorities
Sudden water accumulation in basement apartments

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Move to higher ground immediately.
2. Evacuate the area if ordered by emergency services.
3. Avoid walking or driving through flooded streets.
4. Shut off utility switches and main valves before evacuating.
5. Bring outdoor items indoors if time permits.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not walk through moving water, as even six inches of water can sweep you off your feet.
Do not drive through flooded areas, as cars can float in twelve inches of water.
Do not touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
Do not drink tap water until it is declared safe.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if you are trapped by rising water.
Call if someone is swept away by floodwaters.
Call if there is an active electrical hazard near water.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Monitor local emergency channels for updates and evacuation routes.
Stay on high ground and avoid contact with floodwater, which may contain sewage or chemicals.
Ensure you have fresh drinking water and dry clothing.
Keep your mobile phone charged for emergency updates.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Avoid building structures in flood-prone areas.
Keep gutters and storm drains clear of leaves and debris.
Install check valves in plumbing to prevent floodwater backflow.
Prepare an emergency supply kit with clean drinking water.

==================================================

KEYWORDS

flood, flash flood, rising water, evacuation, high ground, water hazard, contamination, emergency
