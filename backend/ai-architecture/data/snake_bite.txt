==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Snake Bites

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Snake bites can introduce venom into the body, causing tissue damage, internal bleeding, paralysis, or systemic organ failure. Immediate medical treatment with antivenom is necessary. First aid focuses on keeping the victim calm and slowing the spread of venom.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Two puncture marks at the bite site
Severe pain, swelling, or redness around the bite
Difficulty breathing or swallowing
Blurred vision, drooping eyelids, or dizziness
Nausea, vomiting, or sweating

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Move the victim away from the snake's striking distance.
2. Keep the victim calm and still to slow venom spread.
3. Remove any jewelry or tight clothing before swelling starts.
4. Wash the bite gently with soap and water.
5. Keep the bitten limb below the level of the heart.
6. Apply a clean dry bandage loosely over the bite.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not use a tourniquet or constricting band.
Do not cut the wound or try to suck out the venom.
Do not apply ice or water to the bite.
Do not try to capture or kill the snake.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately for all venomous snake bites.
Call if the victim has difficulty breathing or swallowing.
Call if there is rapid swelling, discoloration, or severe pain.
Call if the victim becomes weak or dizzy.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the victim lying down and completely still.
Monitor their breathing and pulse continuously.
Note the color and shape of the snake if visible from a distance.
Write down the exact time of the bite for medical records.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Wear thick leather boots and long pants when walking in tall grass.
Avoid sticking hands or feet into crevices or under rocks.
Use a flashlight when walking outdoors at night in snake country.
Keep lawns trimmed and clear of brush piles.
