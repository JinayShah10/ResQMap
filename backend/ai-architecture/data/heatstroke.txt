==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Heatstroke

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Heatstroke is a severe heat-related illness where the body's internal temperature rises above one hundred and four degrees Fahrenheit. It is highly dangerous because the body loses the ability to sweat and cool itself, leading to rapid damage to the brain, heart, kidneys, and muscles.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

High body temperature above one hundred and four degrees Fahrenheit
Hot, red, dry, or damp skin
Rapid and strong pulse
Confusion, slurred speech, or altered mental state
Headache, dizziness, or unconsciousness

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Move the person to a cool shaded area immediately.
2. Call emergency services without delay.
3. Remove excess or tight clothing layers.
4. Cool the person rapidly using a cool water spray or wet cloths.
5. Apply ice packs to the neck, armpits, and groin.
6. Fan air across the wet skin to speed up cooling.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not use cold water or ice baths for older adults or children.
Do not give the person aspirin or acetaminophen as they do not reduce heat illness.
Do not give fluids to drink if the person is confused or unconscious.
Do not leave the person unattended.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if you suspect someone has heatstroke.
Call if the person is confused, has a seizure, or loses consciousness.
Call if the person is unable to drink fluids or vomits repeatedly.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Continue cooling efforts until the body temperature drops below one hundred and one degrees Fahrenheit.
Monitor the person's breathing and alertness closely.
Keep the person lying flat with legs slightly elevated.
Be prepared to perform CPR if they stop breathing.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Drink plenty of fluids during outdoor activities.
Wear loose, lightweight, and light-colored clothing.
Avoid strenuous exercise during the hottest parts of the day.
Never leave children or pets inside parked cars.

==================================================

KEYWORDS
