==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Action for Fires

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Fires produce intense heat, toxic smoke, and flame, causing severe burns and asphyxiation. The major danger is smoke inhalation, which can cause asphyxiation and lung damage before the flames reach a victim. Immediate evacuation is the most critical survival action.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Thick smoke filling the room or hallway
Visible flames or glowing embers
Crackling sounds or structural collapse
Heat radiating through doors or walls

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Pull the nearest fire alarm to alert others.
2. Evacuate the building immediately using the nearest exit stairwells.
3. Stay low to the ground to avoid inhaling hot toxic smoke.
4. Cover your mouth and nose with a damp cloth if possible.
5. Feel doors with the back of your hand before opening them.
6. Use the stairs instead of elevators during a fire evacuation.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not open a door if it feels hot to the touch because flames may block the other side.
Do not use elevators since power failures can trap you inside.
Do not stop to collect personal belongings during evacuation.
Do not re-enter a burning building under any circumstances.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately once you are in a safe location outside.
Call if someone is trapped inside the building.
Call if the fire is spreading rapidly and cannot be contained.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Meet at a designated assembly point outside the building.
Check that all family members or coworkers are present.
Keep clear of arriving fire vehicles and emergency personnel.
Provide firefighters with information about anyone trapped inside.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Install smoke detectors in every room and test them monthly.
Keep fire extinguishers in the kitchen and garage.
Practice home fire escape drills with all family members.
Keep exit pathways and stairwells clear of obstructions.
