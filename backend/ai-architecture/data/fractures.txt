==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Fractures

==================================================

OVERVIEW

A fracture is a broken or cracked bone resulting from trauma, falls, or direct impact. Fractures can damage surrounding nerves, blood vessels, and soft tissues, leading to internal bleeding or permanent loss of mobility. Immediate immobilization is needed to prevent further damage.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Intense pain at the site of injury
Visible deformity or unnatural angle of the limb
Swelling, bruising, or tenderness
Inability to move the affected body part
Bone protruding through the skin

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Keep the injured person still and avoid moving the broken bone.
2. Cover any open wounds with a sterile dressing.
3. Apply a splint to support the joints above and below the fracture.
4. Apply a cold pack wrapped in a cloth to reduce swelling.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not try to realign or push a protruding bone back into place.
Do not move the victim if a spinal or pelvic fracture is suspected.
Do not apply ice directly to the skin because it causes tissue damage.
Do not allow the victim to eat or drink in case surgery is needed.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if the fracture involves the head, neck, back, or pelvis.
Call if a bone has pierced the skin.
Call if the limb is blue, cold, or numb.
Call if the victim is in severe pain or breathing with difficulty.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the victim warm and comfortable.
Monitor the victim for signs of shock, such as dizziness or shallow breathing.
Keep the splinted limb elevated if it does not cause additional pain.
Reassure the victim regularly.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Keep floors and stairways free of clutter to prevent falls.
Use sturdy ladders when reaching high places.
Wear protective equipment during sports activities.
Install grab bars in bathrooms for older adults.
