==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Action for Building Collapse

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Building collapses are sudden structural failures caused by earthquakes, explosions, fires, or structural weakness. They are highly dangerous because falling concrete, trapping under heavy debris, and dust inhalation can lead to fatal asphyxiation or crush injuries. Immediate evacuation is required.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Loud cracking, groaning, or grinding sounds from structural support beams
Sudden falling of plaster, dust, or ceiling tiles
Doors or windows jamming or refusing to open
Visible buckling of support pillars or walls

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Evacuate the building immediately if you hear cracking sounds or see buckling walls.
2. Drop cover and hold on under a sturdy table if escape is impossible.
3. Cover your face with your shirt to avoid inhaling thick concrete dust.
4. Move away from glass windows and heavy filing cabinets.
5. Exit the building using stairs and avoid elevators.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not use elevators during a structural emergency.
Do not try to retrieve personal items before escaping.
Do not move structural debris if you are trapped, as it may cause further collapse.
Do not scream continuously if trapped, to conserve energy and avoid dust inhalation.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately once you are outside the collapsed building.
Call if you are trapped under debris and have access to a phone.
Call if someone is missing or known to be trapped inside.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Tap on metal pipes or walls to signal your location to rescuers if you are trapped.
Cover your nose and mouth to filter out dust.
Remain calm and conserve your energy.
Listen for the sounds of rescue workers and shout only when they are close.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Conduct regular structural inspections of buildings.
Ensure construction adheres to local building safety codes.
Identify emergency exit routes in every building you enter.
Keep exit pathways clear of storage boxes and debris.

==================================================

KEYWORDS
