==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Action for Lightning

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Lightning is a giant spark of electricity in the atmosphere between clouds or the ground. It is highly dangerous because a direct strike can cause severe burns, permanent brain injury, and instant cardiac arrest. Immediate indoor shelter is the only safe option.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Darkening storm clouds and heavy rain
Audible thunder claps following light flashes
Hair standing on end or static tingling on the skin
Crackling sounds in the air

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Seek shelter immediately inside a fully enclosed building.
2. Get inside a hard-topped metal vehicle if a building is not available.
3. Squat down low with feet together if you are caught outdoors with no shelter.
4. Stay away from tall trees, utility poles, and open fields.
5. Avoid contact with plumbing fixtures and electrical appliances indoors.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not stand under tall trees or isolated poles for shelter.
Do not lie flat on the ground because current can travel through the soil.
Do not use corded phones or wash hands during a lightning storm.
Do not touch metal fences, golf clubs, or bicycles.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if someone is struck by lightning.
Call if the lightning strike causes a fire or structural damage.
Call if the victim is unresponsive or not breathing.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Start CPR immediately if the victim is unresponsive and not breathing.
Cover any visible burns with dry sterile dressings.
Keep the victim warm and lying flat.
Monitor their pulse and breathing continuously.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Follow the thirty-thirty rule: go indoors if thunder is heard within thirty seconds of a flash, and stay inside for thirty minutes after the last thunder.
Install lightning rods on buildings.
Unplug sensitive electronic devices before a storm begins.
