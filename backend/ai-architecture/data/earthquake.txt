TITLE

Emergency Action for Earthquakes

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Earthquakes are sudden shakes of the ground caused by movement of tectonic plates. They are highly dangerous because structural collapse, falling debris, shattered glass, and broken gas lines can cause severe injuries. Immediate protective action is necessary during the shaking.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Violent shaking of the floor and walls
Rattling or breaking of glass windows and mirrors
Falling objects, bookshelves, and light fixtures
Swaying of the building or nearby structures

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Drop down onto your hands and knees immediately.
2. Cover your head and neck with your arms.
3. Crawl under a sturdy table or desk for shelter.
4. Hold on to your shelter until the shaking stops.
5. Move away from glass windows and outside walls.
6. Check yourself and others for injuries once the shaking stops.
7. Evacuate the building carefully using stairs.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not run outside while the ground is shaking as falling debris is highly dangerous.
Do not use elevators because power failures can trap you inside.
Do not stand in doorways as they do not provide protection from falling objects.
Do not turn on lights or appliances in case of a gas leak.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services if someone is trapped under debris or severely injured.
Call if there is a fire or major gas leak inside the building.
Call if power lines have fallen near your location.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Be prepared for aftershocks which can cause further damage.
Monitor local news and emergency broadcasts for safety instructions.
Check gas, water, and electrical lines for damage and shut them off if broken.
Wear sturdy shoes to protect your feet from broken glass.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Secure heavy bookshelves, cabinets, and appliances to the walls.
Store breakable items in low closed cabinets.
Keep an emergency kit containing water, food, and first aid supplies.
Identify safe spots in every room under sturdy furniture.
