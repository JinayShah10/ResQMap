==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Hypothermia

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Hypothermia is a medical emergency that occurs when the body loses heat faster than it can produce it, causing the body temperature to fall below ninety-five degrees Fahrenheit. It is dangerous because a low temperature impairs brain function, leading to confusion and heart failure.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Shivering that cannot be controlled
Slurred speech or mumbling
Slow, shallow breathing and a weak pulse
Clumsiness, lack of coordination, or confusion
Drowsiness or low energy levels

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Move the person to a warm dry shelter immediately.
2. Remove wet clothing layers gently.
3. Warm the center of the body first using blankets or skin-to-skin contact.
4. Provide warm non-alcoholic sweet beverages if the person is fully awake.
5. Keep the person dry and wrapped in warm blankets.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not apply direct heat sources like hot water or heating lamps.
Do not massage or rub the person's limbs as this can trigger cardiac arrest.
Do not give alcohol because it increases internal heat loss.
Do not warm the person too quickly.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if the person shows signs of severe hypothermia.
Call if the person is confused, loses consciousness, or stops shivering.
Call if the person's breathing is very slow or shallow.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Monitor the person's breathing and pulse continuously.
Keep the person wrapped in warm dry blankets including the head.
Keep the person lying flat and still to prevent stress on the heart.
Be prepared to start CPR if they stop breathing.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Dress in multiple layers of warm, windproof clothing.
Wear hats, gloves, and warm socks in cold weather.
Keep clothing dry and change out of wet items quickly.
Avoid prolonged exposure to cold winds and wet conditions.

==================================================

KEYWORDS
