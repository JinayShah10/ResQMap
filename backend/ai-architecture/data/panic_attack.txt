==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Panic Attacks

==================================================

OVERVIEW

A panic attack is a sudden episode of intense fear or anxiety that triggers severe physical reactions. While not directly life-threatening, it is highly distressing and can mimic symptoms of a heart attack or breathing failure. First aid focuses on calming the individual and restoring regular breathing.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Rapid pounding heartbeat or chest tightness
Shortness of breath, hyperventilation, or feeling smothered
Trembling, shaking, or sweating
Dizziness, lightheadedness, or feeling faint
Intense fear of losing control or dying

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Move the person to a quiet calm area away from crowds.
2. Reassure the person that they are safe and that the attack will pass.
3. Encourage the person to breathe slowly and deeply through their nose.
4. Guide them to inhale for four seconds hold for four seconds and exhale for four seconds.
5. Ask them to name five things they can see to ground them.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not tell the person to calm down or tell them their symptoms are in their head.
Do not breathe into a paper bag unless specifically advised by a medical professional.
Do not crowd around the person as this increases anxiety.
Do not leave the person alone during the attack.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services if the chest pain is severe or radiates to the arm or jaw, as this may indicate a heart attack.
Call if the breathing difficulty does not improve with calming techniques.
Call if the person loses consciousness.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Remain calm and speak in a soft gentle voice.
Stay with the person until the attack ends, which usually lasts ten to thirty minutes.
Help them sit down comfortably.
Encourage them to focus on your voice or a simple task.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Practice mindfulness and deep breathing exercises regularly.
Avoid excessive caffeine and alcohol which can trigger anxiety.
Maintain a regular exercise and sleep schedule.
Seek professional counseling or therapy if panic attacks are frequent.
