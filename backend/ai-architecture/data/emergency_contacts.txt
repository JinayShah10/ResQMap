==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Services and Contact Guidelines

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Emergency services are professional teams that respond to critical situations to protect life, health, and property. Knowing when to call each service ensures that the correct assistance arrives quickly, reducing response times and saving lives.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Immediate threat to life or safety
Active fires or structural collapse
Critical medical emergencies or severe injuries
Ongoing crimes or hazardous material spills

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Identify the emergency type (medical, fire, or police).
2. Speak clearly and provide your exact location.
3. Describe what happened and the number of victims.
4. Stay on the line until the operator tells you to hang up.
5. Keep your phone line clear after the call.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not call emergency services for non-emergency questions or inquiries.
Do not hang up if you call by mistake, explain to the operator that it was an accident.
Do not argue with the operator, answer their questions quickly.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call Police immediately for active crimes, motor vehicle accidents, or public safety threats.
Call Fire Brigade for fires, gas leaks, structural collapses, or vehicle entrapment.
Call Ambulance for unresponsive victims, severe bleeding, chest pain, or poisoning.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Follow any medical or safety instructions provided by the operator.
Ensure your location is visible from the road if possible.
Secure pets so they do not interfere with responders.
Gather any relevant medications or identification documents.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Save local emergency numbers in your mobile phone contacts list.
Teach children how to dial emergency services and state their address.
Keep emergency numbers posted on the refrigerator or near home phones.
Learn basic first aid to assist until professional help arrives.
