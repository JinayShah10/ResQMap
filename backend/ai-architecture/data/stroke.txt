TITLE

First Aid for Stroke

==================================================

OVERVIEW

A stroke is a medical emergency that occurs when blood flow to the brain is interrupted or reduced. Without oxygen, brain cells begin to die within minutes, leading to permanent brain damage or disability. Recognizing the signs quickly and getting immediate medical care is critical.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Sudden numbness or weakness in the face, arm, or leg
Sudden confusion or difficulty speaking and understanding speech
Sudden trouble seeing in one or both eyes
Sudden trouble walking, dizziness, or loss of balance
Sudden severe headache with no known cause

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Call emergency services immediately.
2. Ask the person to smile to check for facial droop.
3. Ask the person to raise both arms to check for drift.
4. Ask the person to repeat a simple phrase to check for slurred speech.
5. Note the exact time when symptoms first appeared.
6. Keep the person calm and lying on their side.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not give the person aspirin or any medication as it may worsen bleeding.
Do not give the person food or drink because a stroke impairs swallowing.
Do not wait to see if the symptoms improve on their own.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if the person displays any sign of stroke.
Call even if the symptoms disappear after a few minutes.
Call if the person suddenly collapses or loses consciousness.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the person lying on their side with their head slightly elevated.
Monitor their breathing and pulse continuously.
Reassure the person and explain that help is on the way.
Provide the emergency medical team with the exact onset time of symptoms.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Monitor and manage high blood pressure regularly.
Eat a balanced diet rich in fruits and vegetables.
Engage in regular physical activity.
Avoid smoking and manage chronic conditions like diabetes.
