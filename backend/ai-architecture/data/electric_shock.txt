==================================================

TITLE

First Aid for Electric Shock

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Electric shock occurs when an electrical current passes through the body, causing burns, internal tissue damage, and cardiac arrest. The primary danger is that the heart's electrical rhythm can be disrupted, leading to fatal arrest. Ensuring scene safety is the absolute priority.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Unresponsiveness or loss of consciousness
Burns on the skin where the current entered and exited
Difficulty breathing or absence of breathing
Muscle spasms or seizures

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Do not touch the victim if they are still in contact with the electrical source.
2. Turn off the main power source immediately.
3. Call emergency services and request an automated external defibrillator.
4. Push the electrical source away from the victim using a dry wooden stick if the power cannot be turned off.
5. Check the victim's breathing and start CPR immediately if they are not breathing.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not touch the victim with bare hands if they are touching a live wire.
Do not go near high-voltage power lines until the power company shuts them off.
Do not apply ice or ointment to electrical burns.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately for all cases of electric shock.
Call if the victim is unresponsive or has difficulty breathing.
Call if the shock resulted from high-voltage lines or lightning.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the victim warm and lying flat.
Monitor their breathing and responsiveness continuously.
Cover electrical burns with a clean dry sterile dressing.
Prepare to perform CPR if the victim stops breathing.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Never use electrical appliances with damaged or frayed cords.
Keep electrical appliances away from water and wet surfaces.
Use childproof safety covers on all electrical outlets.
Unplug appliances before cleaning or repairing them.
