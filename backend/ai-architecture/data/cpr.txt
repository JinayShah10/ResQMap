==================================================

TITLE

Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation CPR

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation CPR is an emergency procedure combining chest compressions and rescue breaths. It is performed when a person's heart stops beating or they stop breathing. Immediate CPR keeps oxygenated blood flowing to the brain and vital organs, preventing brain death.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Unresponsiveness when shaken or spoken to
Absence of normal breathing or only gasping
Lack of a pulse or signs of circulation

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Shake the victim gently and shout to check for response.
2. Call emergency services and request an automated external defibrillator.
3. Place the victim on their back on a firm flat surface.
4. Place the heel of one hand on the center of the chest.
5. Interlock your fingers and keep your elbows straight.
6. Push down hard and fast at a rate of one hundred to one hundred and twenty compressions per minute.
7. Open the airway using the head-tilt chin-lift maneuver.
8. Pinch the nose and give two rescue breaths after every thirty compressions.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not stop compressions for more than ten seconds to check for pulse.
Do not press on the ribs or lower tip of the breastbone because they can fracture.
Do not give rescue breaths if you are untrained, perform compression-only CPR.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately if a person of any age is unresponsive and not breathing normally.
Call if a person suddenly collapses and does not wake up.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Continue CPR compressions and breaths in a thirty-to-two ratio.
Use an automated external defibrillator as soon as it becomes available.
Monitor the victim for gasping or movement.
Rotate rescuers every two minutes to prevent fatigue.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Learn CPR from certified emergency medical instructors.
Know the location of automated external defibrillators in public spaces.
Maintain active training credentials annually.
