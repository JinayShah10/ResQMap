==================================================

TITLE

Emergency Action for Cyclones

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Cyclones are intense tropical storms with destructive winds, heavy rain, and storm surges. They are highly dangerous because high winds can destroy buildings, uproot trees, and create flying debris, while storm surges cause severe coastal flooding. Immediate shelter is required.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Extremely high and howling winds
Torrential rainfall causing rapid local flooding
Sudden drop in atmospheric pressure
Severe coastal waves and rising sea levels

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Seek shelter in a sturdy reinforced building immediately.
2. Move to an interior room away from windows and glass doors.
3. Close all windows and secure external doors tightly.
4. Listen to local emergency broadcasts for weather updates.
5. Turn off utility switches and main gas valves if instructed.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not go outside during the calm eye of the storm, as destructive winds will resume suddenly.
Do not shelter in mobile homes or structures with tin roofs.
Do not touch fallen trees or power lines.
Do not drive through storm surges or high winds.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services if your shelter collapses or is severely damaged.
Call if someone is critically injured or trapped by debris.
Call if there is a fire or utility line hazard.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Remain inside your safe shelter until official authorities declare the storm has passed.
Keep your emergency kit, flashlight, and mobile phone close to you.
Monitor water levels around your shelter.
Comfort children and pets to keep them calm.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Construct homes according to cyclone-resistant building codes.
Trim dead branches from trees near the house.
Prepare window shutters and secure loose outdoor objects before the storm season.
Identify the nearest public emergency storm shelter.
