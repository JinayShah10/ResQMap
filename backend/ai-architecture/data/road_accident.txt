TITLE

First Aid for Road Accidents

==================================================

OVERVIEW

Road accidents involve collisions between vehicles, pedestrians, or obstacles. They are highly dangerous due to the risk of severe impact injuries, spinal trauma, vehicle fire, and oncoming traffic hazards. Ensuring scene safety and stabilizing victims are the primary priorities.

==================================================

COMMON SIGNS

Deformed or damaged vehicle bodies
Victims trapped inside or thrown from vehicles
Spilled fuel or smoke rising from engine compartments
Injured persons bleeding or unresponsive on the road

==================================================

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS

1. Park your vehicle at a safe distance and turn on hazard lights.
2. Ensure the scene is safe before approaching the crashed vehicle.
3. Turn off the ignition of the crashed vehicle if possible.
4. Call emergency services immediately.
5. Keep injured persons still, especially if neck or spinal injury is suspected.
6. Control severe bleeding with direct pressure.

==================================================

DO NOT

Do not move the victim unless there is an immediate threat of fire or explosion.
Do not pull a victim's head or neck as it can cause permanent paralysis.
Do not give water or food to injured victims.
Do not attempt to pull a trapped victim from a vehicle without professional rescue tools.

==================================================

WHEN TO CALL EMERGENCY SERVICES

Call emergency services immediately for all road accidents with injuries.
Call if a vehicle is on fire or fuel is spilling.
Call if victims are trapped inside the vehicles.
Call if the accident blocks major highway traffic.

==================================================

UNTIL HELP ARRIVES

Keep the victim warm and calm.
Monitor the victim's breathing and level of consciousness.
Direct oncoming traffic away from the accident scene.
Stay with the victim until professional medical help arrives.

==================================================

PREVENTION

Obey speed limits and traffic signs at all times.
Wear seatbelts and ensure all passengers are buckled.
Never drive under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
Avoid using mobile phones or other distractions while driving.
